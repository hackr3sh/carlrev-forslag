About us

More than an ordinary audit firm.

HLB Carlsson & Partners was founded in 1998 and today consists of auditors, accounting consultants and tax lawyers. Since 2008 we have been part of HLB International, a global network of independent advisory and accounting firms with members in more than 100 countries.

We work in particular with owner-managed companies, international companies with a parent or subsidiary in Sweden, and smaller listed Swedish companies. Today we handle a number of foreign-owned companies from Stockholm.

As our client you always have your own contact person who runs the engagement independently — and who stays with you over the years.
